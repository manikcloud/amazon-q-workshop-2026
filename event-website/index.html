About the Program

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering, SIRT, in association with R&D Cell SGI, is organizing a comprehensive Faculty Development Program focused on cutting-edge AI tools and their applications in research, industry, and education.

Program Objectives:

  • Explore latest AI tools and technologies
  • Hands-on experience with industry applications
  • Research methodologies using AI
  • Educational integration strategies
